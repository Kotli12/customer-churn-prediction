# Customer Churn Prediction

A machine learning project that predicts which customers are likely to cancel their subscription — and tells you exactly what to do about it.

---

## The Business Problem

**57.3% of customers churn every year.** At an average value of $5,211/customer/year, that is **$8.4M in annual revenue walking out the door.**

Acquiring a new customer costs 5–7× more than retaining one. This model identifies at-risk customers *before* they cancel, so the business can intervene in time.

## What Each Script Does

### `eda.py` — Understand the data first

Produces 4 charts, each answering a business question:

| Chart | Question |
|---|---|
| `01_churn_crisis.png` | How critical is our churn problem? |
| `02_who_is_leaving.png` | Which customer segments are we losing? |
| `03_warning_signs.png` | What are the early warning signs? |
| `04_correlation_heatmap.png` | Which metrics are most connected to churn? |

---

### `train.py` — Build the model

Tests 5 models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM) and picks the best one. Every decision is explained in business terms as it runs:

- Why these features were chosen
- Why each model type was tested
- Why we optimise for Recall over Accuracy
- What the projected business ROI is

**Why Recall matters more than Accuracy:**

| Error type | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| False Negative (miss a churner) | Customer leaves uncontacted | $5,211 lost/year |
| False Positive (false alarm) | Unnecessary outreach call | ~$50 |

The asymmetry is clear — catching more churners is always worth a few extra calls.

---

### `predict.py` — Score customers

Translates predictions into business actions:

```
🔴  User 9001  |  Plan: Premium  |  Churn probability: 98.3%  |  Risk: High
    Annual value : $8,388  |  Revenue at risk: $8,245
    Action       : [HIGH] Personal outreach + retention offer  (est. cost: $300)
      → Inactive 30+ days — send re-engagement campaign immediately
      → 3+ payment failures — offer payment plan or billing support
```

## Model Performance

| Model | Accuracy | Recall | ROC-AUC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Random Forest ✓ | 64.6% | 67.6% | 0.722 |
| Logistic Regression | 64.5% | 63.9% | 0.712 |
| LightGBM | 64.1% | 67.8% | 0.711 |
| Gradient Boosting | 62.9% | 70.1% | 0.705 |
| XGBoost | 62.7% | 62.6% | 0.702 |

**ROC-AUC of 0.722** means the model correctly ranks a real churner above a non-churner 72% of the time.
